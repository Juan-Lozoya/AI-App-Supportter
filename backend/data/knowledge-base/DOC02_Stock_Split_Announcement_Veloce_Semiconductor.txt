1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY AND EVENT TERMS
The Board of Directors of Veloce Semiconductor Corporation ("Veloce" or the "Company") has approved a 5-for-2 forward stock split of its authorized and issued Common Stock. Under the terms of the split, each holder of record at the close of business on September 18, 2026, will receive 2.5 shares of post-split Common Stock for each 1 share of pre-split Common Stock held (an incremental distribution of 1.5 additional shares per existing share).

In conjunction with this corporate action, Veloce Semiconductor Corporation is executing a mandatory security identifier change. Pre-split shares trading under ISIN US92264B1052 (CUSIP 92264B105) will be retired and replaced on a 2.5-for-1 basis with post-split shares carrying ISIN US92264B2043 (CUSIP 92264B204). The NASDAQ ticker symbol "VLCE" will remain unchanged.

3. SHARE QUANTITY ADJUSTMENT EXAMPLES AND FRACTIONAL TREATMENT
3.1 Calculation Methodology
Because the split ratio is 5-for-2 (multiplier = 2.5), any shareholder holding an odd number of pre-split shares will generate a fractional entitlement of 0.5 shares.
- New Total Position = Old Settled Position * 2.5
- Incremental Shares Issued = Old Settled Position * 1.5

3.2 Treatment of Fractional Shares (Cash-in-Lieu)
No fractional shares of Veloce Semiconductor Corporation Common Stock will be issued or registered on the books of the transfer agent (Equiniti Trust Company). All fractional entitlements resulting from the 5-for-2 split will be aggregated at the central depository (DTC) level, sold in the open market by the transfer agent on September 28, 2026, and settled as Cash-in-Lieu (CIL) in USD on October 5, 2026. The CIL reference price will be determined by the volume-weighted average price (VWAP) of post-split VLCE shares on September 28, 2026.

4. IMPACT ON HOLDINGS, COST BASIS, AND OPEN ORDERS
- Market Value Neutrality: On the Effective Date (September 28, 2026), the theoretical market price per share of VLCE will adjust to 40% (2/5ths) of its closing price on September 25, 2026, keeping aggregate portfolio market value unchanged prior to intraday market fluctuations.
- Cost Basis Allocation: Total historical tax cost basis must be preserved across the new whole shares (adjusted slightly downward for any fraction sold for Cash-in-Lieu, which triggers a minor realized capital gain/loss event on the 0.5 fractional share). Per-share cost basis is divided by 2.5.
- Open Orders & Derivatives: All Good-Til-Canceled (GTC) limit orders on the execution desk for VLCE will be automatically canceled by NASDAQ after market close on September 25, 2026. Listed options contracts (OCC) will undergo standard contract adjustments (deliverable becomes 250 shares per contract with strike prices divided by 2.5).

Because the Ex-Date (September 28) occurs after the Record Date (September 18), a five-business-day Due-Bill window applies.
- Any client purchasing VLCE between September 21 and September 25 (inclusive) is entitled to the 1.5 incremental split shares even though they were not on the books on September 18.

3. Book any 0.5 fractional remainder into the Fractional CIL Receivable Suspense Account (`SUSP-CIL-VLCE`) pending cash receipt on October 5, 2026.
